About this verbal memory test

This verbal memory test is the classic "how many words can you remember" challenge popularized by Human Benchmark. One word is shown at a time. Early on almost everything is new, so you press NEW and the word is quietly added to your growing "seen" list. As the run goes on, words start to repeat and you have to recognize them and press SEEN. Every correct answer adds a point; every wrong answer — a repeat you called NEW, or a fresh word you called SEEN — costs one of your three lives. When all three are gone the game ends and shows your score.

The tool keeps the whole seen list in memory using a JavaScript Set, picks the next word with a simple heuristic that shows repeats more often as your seen list grows, and stores your best score in your browser with localStorage so nothing is sent anywhere. It works with a mouse, touch, or the keyboard, needs no signup, and runs entirely client-side. An average casual score is around 40; consistently clearing 60 is strong and usually means you are chunking words or tracking patterns rather than memorizing a flat list.

언어 기억력 테스트 소개

이 언어 기억력 테스트는 Human Benchmark로 널리 알려진 "몇 개의 단어를 기억할 수 있는가" 챌린지입니다. 한 번에 한 단어가 표시됩니다. 초반에는 대부분 처음 보는 단어이므로 NEW를 누르고, 그 단어는 "본 단어" 목록에 조용히 추가됩니다. 판이 진행되면 단어가 반복되기 시작하고, 그것을 알아보고 SEEN을 눌러야 합니다. 정답마다 1점씩 올라가고, 오답(반복된 단어를 NEW로, 새 단어를 SEEN으로)마다 생명 3개 중 하나를 잃습니다. 3개가 모두 사라지면 게임이 끝나고 점수가 표시됩니다.

단어 목록은 영어로 고정되어 있습니다. 이것이 이 테스트의 표준 방식이며, KO 사용자도 단어의 뜻을 알 필요 없이 "전에 나왔는지"만 판단하면 됩니다. 툴은 본 단어 전체를 JavaScript Set에 저장하고, 목록이 커질수록 반복 단어를 더 자주 보여주는 간단한 휴리스틱으로 다음 단어를 고르며, 최고 점수는 localStorage로 브라우저에만 저장합니다. 마우스·터치·키보드 모두 지원하고 가입이 필요 없으며 전부 브라우저에서 실행됩니다. 평균은 약 40점, 꾸준히 60점을 넘기면 우수한 편입니다.

How to use this verbal memory test

1. Start the run

Press Start. A single word appears in the centre of the box with your three lives shown above it.

2. Judge each word

Press NEW if this is the first time the word has appeared this run, or SEEN if it has shown up before. Use the buttons, or Left/K for NEW and Right/L for SEEN.

3. Protect your lives

Every correct call scores a point. A wrong call loses a life. The game keeps going until all three lives are gone.

4. Read your score

At zero lives you see your score, how it compares (average is around 40; over 60 is strong), and your saved best. Press Try again to start a fresh run.

언어 기억력 테스트 사용 방법

1. 판 시작

Start를 누르세요. 박스 중앙에 단어 하나가 나타나고 위에 생명 3개가 표시됩니다.

2. 각 단어 판단

이번 판에서 처음 보는 단어면 NEW, 전에 나온 적 있으면 SEEN을 누르세요. 버튼을 쓰거나, NEW는 왼쪽 화살표·K, SEEN은 오른쪽 화살표·L.

3. 생명 지키기

정답마다 1점. 오답마다 생명 하나. 생명 3개가 모두 사라질 때까지 계속됩니다.

4. 결과 확인

생명이 0이 되면 점수, 비교(평균 약 40, 60 이상이면 우수), 저장된 최고 기록이 표시됩니다. Try again으로 새 판을 시작하세요.

Frequently asked questions

On this style of test a score in the 40s is around average for adults. Reaching 50 to 70 is a solid result, and scores above 70 or 80 usually mean you are using a deliberate strategy such as chunking words into groups or noticing patterns in which words repeat. Because the word pool is limited, very high scores also depend on stamina and focus rather than raw memory alone.
It measures verbal working memory and recognition memory: your ability to hold a growing list of words in mind and quickly decide whether a new word is one you have already seen. It also taps sustained attention, because a single lapse late in the game ends the run. It is not a full clinical memory assessment, but it is a reasonable proxy for how well you keep verbal information active over several minutes.
For casual players the average is roughly 40 correct answers. Most people land somewhere between 25 and 55. Scores drift up with practice as you learn to pace yourself and adopt a memory strategy, and they drop when you are tired, distracted, or rushing your answers. Treat any single result as a rough snapshot and compare your own scores over time rather than against other people.
Practice helps in the short term: slow down, read each word deliberately, and group words into small clusters instead of trying to memorize a flat list. Over the longer term, sleep, aerobic exercise, and reducing divided attention (fewer notifications, single-tasking) support working memory more than brain-training apps do. Reading regularly and learning new vocabulary also keep verbal recall sharp.
The standard version of this test, popularized by Human Benchmark, uses a fixed pool of common English words so that scores are comparable between players. We keep the English word list for the same reason, and because swapping languages would change word length and familiarity and make results incomparable. Non-native English speakers can still take the test; recognizing whether a word has appeared before does not require knowing its meaning.
