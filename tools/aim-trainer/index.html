About this aim trainer

This aim trainer is a browser version of the classic "click the dot" accuracy test. When you start a run, a circular target appears at a random spot inside the play area. Click or tap it and it instantly respawns somewhere else. The timer starts on your first hit and stops when you clear the last target, so your headline score is the average time per target in milliseconds. It also reports total time, misses, and accuracy so you can see whether you are trading precision for speed.

It is built for quick daily warm-ups and for testing a new mouse, sensitivity, or mousepad. Choose 10 targets for a fast check, or 30 to 50 for a proper endurance run. Your best average per target count is saved in your browser so you can chase it. Everything runs client-side with no signup, and it works with a mouse on desktop or touch on a phone or tablet.

How to use this aim trainer

Step 1
Pick a target count
Choose 10 for a quick check, or 30 to 50 for an endurance run. 10 is the default.
Step 2
Start the run
Click Start. The first target appears at a random position. The timer begins the moment you land your first hit.
Step 3
Clear every target
Click each target as fast as you can. Every hit respawns a new one. Clicks that miss the target are counted against your accuracy.
Step 4
Read your score
See your average ms per target, total time, misses and accuracy. Hit Try again to reset and beat your saved best.

Frequently asked questions

On a click-the-target aim trainer, average time per target is the number that matters. Sub-600 ms per target with high accuracy is a solid result for a casual player. Regular FPS players tend to land around 450 to 550 ms, and competitive players often hit near 400 ms while keeping accuracy above 90 percent. Speed alone is not the goal; a fast average with lots of misses is worse than a slightly slower average with clean hits.
Practice short daily sessions rather than one long grind, and warm up before you measure. Lower your mouse sensitivity so a full crosshair sweep uses most of your mousepad; most players aim best somewhere between 30 and 50 cm per 360 degree turn. Use a large mousepad, a consistent desk setup, and pull from the elbow for big flicks and the wrist for small corrections. Focus on stopping precisely on the target instead of slashing past it, and let accuracy lead speed.
For this trainer, under 600 ms per target is good, 450 to 550 ms is strong, and consistently under 400 ms is excellent and comparable to competitive FPS players. Times above 800 ms usually mean the sensitivity is too high or too low, the grip is unstable, or you are hunting for the next target instead of tracking where it appeared. Screen size and refresh rate also shift the number by a few tens of milliseconds.
Effective sensitivity matters, DPI on its own does not. What counts is centimeters per 360 degree turn, which is DPI multiplied by the in-game or in-app sensitivity. Very high sensitivity makes small corrections twitchy and inconsistent; very low sensitivity makes you run out of mousepad. Pick a comfortable effective sensitivity, keep it identical across games and trainers, and change it rarely so your muscle memory can settle.
A browser aim trainer like this one is great for a quick warm-up, for tracking a simple click-speed-and-accuracy number over time, and for testing a new mouse or sensitivity. Dedicated apps such as Aim Lab and Kovaak's add tracking, flicking, target switching, and detailed analytics that a lightweight web tool does not. Use the online trainer for fast daily reps and the desktop apps when you want structured drills and progress graphs.

자주 묻는 질문

타겟 클릭 방식 에임 트레이너에서는 타겟당 평균 시간이 핵심 지표입니다. 높은 정확도로 타겟당 600ms 미만이면 캐주얼 플레이어 기준 좋은 결과입니다. 일반 FPS 플레이어는 대략 450~550ms, 경쟁 플레이어는 정확도 90% 이상을 유지하며 400ms에 근접하는 경우가 많습니다. 속도만이 목표는 아닙니다. 놓친 클릭이 많은 빠른 평균보다 깔끔하게 명중한 조금 느린 평균이 낫습니다.
한 번에 길게 하기보다 매일 짧게 연습하고, 측정 전 워밍업하세요. 크로스헤어를 크게 움직일 때 마우스패드 대부분을 쓰도록 감도를 낮추세요. 대부분 360도 회전당 30~50cm에서 가장 잘 조준합니다. 큰 마우스패드와 일관된 책상 세팅을 쓰고, 큰 플릭은 팔꿈치, 작은 보정은 손목으로 하세요. 타겟을 지나쳐 휘두르지 말고 정확히 멈추는 데 집중하며 정확도가 속도를 이끌게 하세요.
이 트레이너에서는 타겟당 600ms 미만이면 좋고, 450~550ms면 우수하며, 꾸준히 400ms 미만이면 경쟁 FPS 플레이어 수준으로 탁월합니다. 800ms를 넘으면 보통 감도가 너무 높거나 낮고, 그립이 불안정하거나, 타겟이 나타난 곳을 보지 않고 다음 타겟을 찾고 있다는 뜻입니다. 화면 크기와 주사율도 수십 ms 정도 결과를 바꿉니다.
실효 감도가 중요하며 DPI 자체는 그렇지 않습니다. 중요한 것은 360도 회전당 센티미터이며, 이는 DPI에 게임 또는 앱 내 감도를 곱한 값입니다. 감도가 너무 높으면 작은 보정이 예민하고 불안정해지고, 너무 낮으면 마우스패드가 부족해집니다. 편안한 실효 감도를 정해 모든 게임과 트레이너에서 동일하게 유지하고, 근육 기억이 자리 잡도록 거의 바꾸지 마세요.
이런 브라우저 에임 트레이너는 빠른 워밍업, 클릭 속도와 정확도라는 단순 지표를 시간에 따라 추적하는 것, 새 마우스나 감도 테스트에 좋습니다. Aim Lab이나 Kovaak's 같은 전용 앱은 트래킹, 플리킹, 타겟 전환, 상세 분석을 제공하는데 가벼운 웹 툴에는 없습니다. 빠른 매일 반복은 온라인 트레이너를, 체계적인 훈련과 진행 그래프가 필요할 때는 데스크톱 앱을 사용하세요.
