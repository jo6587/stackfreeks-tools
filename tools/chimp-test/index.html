About the Chimp Test

The Chimp Test is named after a famous 2007 study by Kyoto University primatologist Tetsuro Matsuzawa, in which a young chimpanzee named Ayumu was shown to memorize and recall the positions of numbers on a touchscreen faster and more accurately than human adults. This online version recreates that task: a grid of tiles shows a set of numbers for a brief moment, the numbers then vanish and every tile looks identical, and you have to click the tiles back in ascending order (1, 2, 3...) purely from spatial memory.

Each successful round adds one more numbered tile to the grid, so the challenge scales automatically until you make a mistake. One wrong click ends the run, and your score is the round you reached. The game loads instantly, works with mouse or touch, keeps your best round in your browser's local storage, and never sends any data to a server.

How to play the Chimp Test

1. Press Start

A grid appears with a few tiles showing numbers, starting at 1. Study their positions closely.

2. Watch them disappear

After a short moment every tile is masked to look identical — the numbers are gone from view, not from your memory.

3. Click in order

Click the tiles that used to hold 1, 2, 3 and so on, purely from memory. Each correct click reveals that number briefly.

4. Advance or retry

Finish the sequence and the next round adds one more tile. One wrong click ends the run — press Try again to restart from round 1.

침팬지 테스트 플레이 방법

1. 시작 누르기

몇 개의 타일에 1부터 시작하는 숫자가 표시된 격자가 나타납니다. 위치를 잘 살펴보세요.

2. 사라지는 숫자 관찰

잠시 후 모든 타일이 똑같이 가려집니다. 숫자는 화면에서 사라졌을 뿐, 당신의 기억 속에서는 아닙니다.

3. 순서대로 클릭

기억만으로 1, 2, 3... 순서에 해당했던 타일을 클릭하세요. 맞을 때마다 그 숫자가 잠깐 표시됩니다.

4. 다음 라운드 또는 재시도

순서를 모두 맞히면 타일이 하나 더 늘어난 다음 라운드로 넘어갑니다. 하나라도 틀리면 게임이 끝나며, 다시 시도를 눌러 라운드 1부터 재시작합니다.

Frequently asked questions

The Chimp Test is a short-term memory game based on a study by primatologist Tetsuro Matsuzawa at Kyoto University. A young chimpanzee named Ayumu was trained to remember the positions of numbers on a touchscreen, and in several trials he outperformed human adults at the same task. This online version recreates the test: numbered tiles flash briefly on a grid, then get hidden, and you click them back in ascending order from memory.
Researchers believe young chimpanzees may have a form of eidetic-like, near-photographic short-term memory that fades with age, similar to how young children sometimes show strong visual recall. Humans tend to rely more on verbal labeling and sequential rehearsal, which is slower for this kind of spatial snapshot task. It is not that chimps are smarter overall, just that this specific memory channel appears sharper in young chimps than in adult humans.
Reaching a round with 8 to 10 numbered tiles is a solid human result, and most people plateau somewhere between 5 and 9. In the original research, the chimpanzee Ayumu correctly recalled sequences of around 9 numbers flashed for under a second, which is why beating a high round count is considered genuinely impressive for a human player.
When a round starts, the numbered tiles show their digits for a short window so you can study their positions. After that window ends, every tile is replaced with a blank tile of identical appearance, so you can no longer tell which tiles held which numbers by looking. You then have to click the tiles in order from 1 upward relying entirely on your memory of where each number was.
Try grouping the tile positions into a shape or path instead of memorizing each one separately, focus on the corners and edges first since they are easier to anchor, and glance at the whole grid rather than fixating on one tile. Playing several short rounds in a row also helps, since this kind of visuospatial memory improves quickly with repeated practice even though large permanent gains are unlikely.

숫자 타일 8~10개인 라운드에 도달하면 인간으로서 좋은 결과이며, 대부분의 사람은 5~9 사이에서 정체됩니다. 원래 연구에서 침팬지 아유무는 1초도 안 되는 시간 동안 표시된 약 9개의 숫자 순서를 정확히 기억해냈는데, 이것이 인간이 높은 라운드에 도달하는 것이 인상적으로 여겨지는 이유입니다.

타일 위치를 각각 따로 외우기보다 하나의 모양이나 경로로 묶어보고, 모서리와 가장자리부터 먼저 기준점으로 삼으세요. 한 타일에 집중하기보다 격자 전체를 훑어보는 것도 도움이 됩니다. 짧은 라운드를 연달아 플레이하는 것도 도움이 되는데, 이런 시공간 기억력은 큰 영구적 향상은 어렵더라도 반복 연습으로 빠르게 좋아지는 경향이 있기 때문입니다.
